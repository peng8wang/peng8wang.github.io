Optimization for Deep Learning (OptDL) Lab

Introduction

  • OptDL Lab is dedicated to advancing the theoretical foundations and practical methodologies of optimization in modern machine learning. Our research lies at the intersection of optimization, deep learning, and AI, aiming to understand and improve the training, generalization, and interpretability of deep learning and AI models.
